What is the difference between mediation and arbitration?

In mediation, an impartial mediator helps people communicate and explore an agreement together. In arbitration, an arbitrator hears both sides and makes a decision according to the agreed process.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Start with a consultation. We can discuss your situation and explain the available services so you can decide on your next step.

Where do you provide services?

We serve individuals, families, workplaces, and communities throughout the Greater Toronto Area. Contact us to discuss appointment arrangements.

How do I prepare for my first conversation?

Think about your main concerns and what you hope to achieve. You can also visit our Forms page. We will let you know which documents are relevant to your process.
